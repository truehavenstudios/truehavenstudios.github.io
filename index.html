Sign in with Google – why and what we access

The full versions of our apps ask you to sign in with Google. We use it for one purpose only: to save a backup copy of your own app data (for example your workouts or your job applications) in your own Google Drive, so it is never lost and stays in sync between your phone and your computer.

  • We request only the scope drive.appdata. It gives the app access to a single hidden app folder in your Drive that only the app itself can use.
  • The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files, your emails, contacts or calendar.
  • Your data goes directly from your device to Google. It is never sent to us, never sold or shared, and not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • You can sign out at any time in the app's settings and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
