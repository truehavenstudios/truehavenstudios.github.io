Privacy policy

TrueHaven Studios – applies to the Fitness Planner and the Job Application Tracker web apps. Last updated: October 5, 2026.

Summary

Our apps run entirely in your browser. Everything you enter is stored on your device and – after you sign in with Google – in your own Google Drive. There is no account with us and no server of ours that receives your data. We cannot see your data.

What data the apps handle

  • Fitness Planner: your settings, goal, workouts, daily numbers (steps, sleep, weight, water, protein), habits, body measurements and optional cycle dates.
  • Job Application Tracker: your settings, job applications (company, role, status, dates, salary, contact details you enter, notes, saved job ads), interviews and interview notes, offers and job search activities.

This data is entered by you and stays in your browser storage on your device.

Sign in with Google and Google Drive

The full versions of the apps ask you to sign in with Google. The apps request only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. With it, the app saves one file with your app data (listed above) in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. We use this only to back up your data and keep it in sync between your devices.

  • The app can only access this one app folder – not your other Drive files, your emails, contacts or calendar.
  • The data goes directly from your device to Google. We never receive, store, share or sell it.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train or improve AI or machine learning models.
  • No humans read your data.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Apple Health (Fitness Planner, optional)

An optional Apple Shortcut reads today's steps, distance, floors and active calories from Apple Health on your iPhone and opens the planner with these numbers. They go straight to the planner on your device – not to us.

Calendar and email (Job Application Tracker)

Calendar reminders are created as a file you download or as a Google Calendar link you open yourself. Email templates open in your own mail app. The app never sends anything on its own and does not access your calendar or email account.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Start fresh deletes your data on the device and in the Drive copy.
  • Sign out any time in Settings and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the Drive file: Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › select the app › Options › Delete hidden app data.

Cookies and tracking

We use no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
